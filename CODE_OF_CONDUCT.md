# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, technical, and respectful.
- Critique code and designs, not people.
- Assume good faith and ask for evidence when a claim is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of harassment or discrimination.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and deliberate
disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the repository's security contact. Maintainers
may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts that violate this policy.
